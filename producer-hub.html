About this trial

Cutting methane from Tasmanian livestock, while keeping farms profitable.

Asparagopsis seaweed-based supplements have demonstrated considerable promise in reducing livestock methane emissions under controlled research settings. What remains untested at a commercial scale in Tasmania is whether those benefits hold up on working farms, and at what cost. That is what this trial is testing.

Backed by a $4 million grant from the Tasmanian Government, TasFarmers partnered with the Tasmanian Institute of Agriculture, and with Sea Forest as the manufacturer of the supplement, to run the trial on participating farms and capture the data.

Every participating farm's results, cost data and herd performance feed directly into this trial. The data your farm generates is what will let every other Tasmanian livestock producer decide, on real evidence rather than a promise, whether this is worth adopting on their own place.
